So, work has really ramped up. I've become quite busy with it, and in the very near future, I can see it taking up a very large portion of my brain, as well as my time. That's okay, but it does mean that I won't be able to spend as much time working on personal projects as I would like.. At least everything I'm working on is in go, and I'm learning more everyday right now than I probably ever have writing code! Overall, it's such a phenomenal opportunity that I have, working with so many talented people on such interesting, big problems. By the end of all of this, I should be a much, much better developer than I have been in the past.

In some ways, it's like some kind of little curse or something. It's like, to become great at the thing I want to do, I have to devote all of my time and energy to it, mostly building things that don't directly benefit me, or that maybe I'm not as invested in, but nothing comes for free, and I think it's a relatively small price to pay for all of the knowledge I've gained so far. Not to discredit money, as well, but there's a million ways to make money, only so many ways to surround yourself with people who are passionate, talented, intelligent people in the same field.

All in all, I'm pretty excited about where stuff is going, but also sort of exhausted at the same time. The plan is the same as usual: try to work twice as hard and twice as long as everybody around me, until I rise above a moderate level.
